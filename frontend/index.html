Welcome 👋

Two quick checks open C-Lab: your email and ID. The rest is optional and you can finish it later.

  1. Verify your email required
    We send a 6-digit code to your login email. It works for 15 minutes.
  2. Your SA ID number required
    Checked with the Luhn check digit, stored encrypted, and used to open your statements.
  3. EasyEquities login optional
    Read-only: C-Lab reads your accounts and holdings. Stored encrypted.
  4. Google (Gmail) optional
    Read-only Gmail: your EasyEquities emails and bank statements.
  5. Statements open with your ID optional
    Proof it all works: C-Lab finds at least one EasyEquities or bank statement and opens it with your ID.
